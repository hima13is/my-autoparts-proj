# Product photo guide for suppliers

_Share this with each supplier. Good photos = more sales for both of us._

## Must-have photos for every product (minimum 4)

| #   | Photo                                          | Tips                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Main photo**: product alone, front view      | Plain **white or light-grey** background (a white bedsheet or chart paper works). The product fills most of the frame.                |
| 2   | **Back / side view**                           | Same background.                                                                                                                      |
| 3   | **Close-up of material / stitching / texture** | Show quality: leather grain, fabric weave, buttons, logo.                                                                             |
| 4   | **Fitted on a real car**                       | Seat cover on the seat, mat on the floor, etc. Write down **which car model and year** it is in. This builds trust and helps fitment. |
| 5   | _(nice to have)_ **What's in the box**         | All pieces laid out neatly (covers, clips, hooks, manual).                                                                            |
| 6   | _(nice to have)_ **Size / scale**              | Next to a ruler or a hand, for items like dashcams and perfumes.                                                                      |

## Camera settings (phone is fine)

- Use the **main back camera**, not the selfie camera. Clean the lens first.
- **Daylight** near a window, or outdoors in shade. No harsh sun, **no flash**.
- Hold the phone **straight** (not tilted). Tap the product on screen to focus.
- Square photos are best: set the camera to **1:1** if available, or leave space around the product.
- At least **1200 × 1200 pixels**. Any modern phone is fine; **don't** send through WhatsApp "compressed". Send as **Document** or by Google Drive/email so quality isn't lost.

## Please avoid

- ❌ Photos copied from other websites, Amazon/Flipkart listings or brand catalogues (copyright problems)
- ❌ Watermarks, your shop name/phone number, price stickers, text written on the photo
- ❌ Cluttered backgrounds (other products, people, floor tiles)
- ❌ Blurry, dark or yellow-tinted photos
- ❌ Collages (several photos stitched into one)

## How to name and send

Put each product in its own folder named with your **product code / SKU**, e.g. `SC-CRETA-BLK/`, containing `1-main.jpg`, `2-back.jpg`, `3-closeup.jpg`, `4-on-car.jpg` …
For colour variants, one folder per colour: `SC-CRETA-BLK`, `SC-CRETA-BEIGE`.

Along with photos, please send for each product: **title, brand, material, colour(s), size, which cars it fits (make, model, years, variant)**, what's in the box, MRP, your price to us, HSN code and GST rate, and the weight/size of the packed box (for shipping).

## Permission

By sending photos, the supplier confirms they own them (or have the right to share them) and allow FitGaadi to use them on its website, ads and social media for products bought from that supplier.
